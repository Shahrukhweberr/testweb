OUR STORY

More than an aquarium.

AquaWorld was created to help people experience the wonder of the ocean while inspiring them to protect it.

WHO WE ARE

A place where curiosity meets conservation.

From our first small reef exhibit to today’s immersive ocean galleries, AquaWorld has grown around one simple idea: when people understand the ocean, they care more deeply about its future.

Our aquarists, educators, researchers and animal-care teams work together to create meaningful experiences for visitors while putting animal welfare and conservation at the heart of everything we do.

Our mission

To connect people with aquatic life, advance marine knowledge and inspire practical action for healthier oceans.

WHAT GUIDES US

Three things we believe in.

💙

Care deeply
